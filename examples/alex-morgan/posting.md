# Senior Python Engineer, Data Platform — Fieldstone Analytics

> Fictional job posting used by the tailored-cv demo.
> Source: `https://jobs.example.com/fieldstone/senior-python-engineer`

**Location:** Remote · EU (two on-site days per quarter in Lisbon)
**Type:** Full-time · Senior

## About Fieldstone

Fieldstone Analytics helps mid-size retailers forecast demand. Our data platform ingests point-of-sale, inventory and logistics events from 400+ stores and turns them into forecasts our customers check every morning.

## What you'll do

- Design and own Python services on our data platform, from API to database.
- Build and operate event-driven pipelines that move store data into the warehouse.
- Make the platform observable: tracing, metrics and alerts that tell us what broke before customers do.
- Mentor engineers and raise the bar on design reviews.

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years building backend services in **Python**, ideally with **FastAPI**
- Strong **PostgreSQL**: schema design, partitioning, query tuning
- Running services on **Kubernetes** in **AWS**
- **Event-driven** architectures (Kafka or similar)
- Observability with **OpenTelemetry** and Prometheus
- Experience **mentoring** other engineers
- Nice to have: Go, dbt

## What we offer

- Fully remote within the EU, with two on-site days per quarter in Lisbon
- Learning budget and conference time
